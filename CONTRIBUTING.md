# Contributing to HomeBracket

Thanks for your interest! HomeBracket is a young, personal open-source project and help is welcome.

## Ways to help

- **Test photos:** bracketed RAW sets of interiors/exteriors you are allowed to share (state camera, lens and bracket settings).
- **Code:** pipeline modules (grouping, alignment, merge, white balance, vertical correction, lens correction, export) and the comparison bench.
- **Docs:** setup guides, tips for specific cameras and lenses, translations.
- **Ideas and comparisons:** results from other open-source tools on the same scenes.

## Ground rules

- Open an issue before large changes so we can agree on the approach.
- Keep pull requests small and focused; describe what changed and how you checked it.
- Only open-source or free dependencies; no paid services or proprietary SDKs in the core pipeline.
- Do not upload photos with identifiable people, addresses, or private documents.
- Be kind and constructive.

By contributing you agree your work is released under the MIT license.
